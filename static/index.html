In 2024, Indians lost ₹22,845 crore to cyber fraud, three times the year before. A scam only has to look real for a few seconds.

Figures: Minister of State for Home Affairs, written reply in the Lok Sabha, 22 July 2025 (₹22,845.73 crore in 2024, ₹7,465.18 crore in 2023), reported by PTI.

Three possible answers. One of them is "can't say".

  • Likely scam

    The evidence points to fraud. You see which sources say so, and what to do right now.

  • Can't say

    The evidence is thin or conflicting. We tell you that instead of guessing, and what you can check yourself.

  • Likely genuine

    The evidence points to a real sender, such as an official domain with a long history and no flags.

What happens to your message

  1. Read

    We pull out the links, phone numbers and UPI ids, and what the message asks you to do. Hidden instructions inside it are ignored.

  2. Check reputation

    Each link goes to blocklists and registries at the same time: is it reported for phishing, how old is the domain, does it imitate a brand?

  3. Visit, sealed

    If the answers are unclear, our server opens the link in a locked-down browser, follows every redirect and reads what the page asks for.

  4. Judge, with receipts

    The verdict may only cite entries in the ledger. When the evidence is thin, it says it can't say, instead of guessing.
